Stay policies

Guests & children

  • 3rd adult (extra bed): 70% of adult rate.
  • Guests 18 years and above must present ID or passport at check-in.
  • Children 2–12 sharing parents’ room: 50% of adult rate.
  • Children 2–12 in own room: 80% of adult rate.
  • Children must present identification: passport copy or birth certificate.
  • Children 13 up to 18 years must also present a passport copy or birth certificate.

Payment & tariff

  • Full payment in advance.
  • Rates are subject to change without prior notice.
